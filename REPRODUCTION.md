## The CRLB acceptance criterion — corrected

`CLAUDE.md` and the project brief both state: *"The published campaign recovered all
four at 0.99–1.02× CRLB. That is the acceptance criterion."*

**No four-parameter efficiency table exists in any legacy report.** The 0.99–1.02×
figure is from a **two-parameter** table on the **older single-exponential** truth
model, covering 6 of its 8 cells (the others are 1.13× and 0.68×). See AUDIT.md §5.1.

Worse, a ±5 % band on this ratio is **not achievable at 10 seeds by anyone**. The
ratio is a sample statistic. For a half-normal error distribution its standard error
is `0.7555/√n` — **±0.239 at n=10**, roughly five times the band being asked for.

Measured on Model C at σ = 0.5 K, n = 17, folded convention:

| seeds | SE | Δθ_or | τ_o | Δθ_hr | τ_w |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 10 | ±0.239 | 0.755 | 1.240 | 1.299 | 0.831 |
| 50 | ±0.107 | 0.978 | 0.974 | 0.919 | 0.888 |
| 100 | ±0.076 | 1.027 | 1.004 | 1.020 | 0.963 |
| 200 | ±0.053 | 1.003 | 1.013 | 0.935 | 0.994 |
| **400** | **±0.038** | **0.973** | **1.010** | **0.945** | **0.965** |

The 10-seed scatter was entirely sampling noise. **At 400 seeds all four parameters
sit on the Cramér–Rao bound**, and the signed bias is ≤ 0.11 % — which is the
precondition for the bound to apply at all.

**This is a stronger claim than the one it replaces**, and unlike that one it is
computed on the production model, at a seed count that can actually resolve it, and
reproducible from this repository.

**New four-parameter CRLB for Model C** (σ = 0.5 K, n = 17) — published here for the
first time; the legacy 0.09 / 0.43 / 0.64 / 3.90 % belongs to the old model:

| parameter | CRLB |
|---|---|
| Δθ_or | 0.079 % |
| τ_o | 0.757 % |
| Δθ_hr | 0.631 % |
| τ_w | 2.971 % |

ρ(τ_o, τ_w) = **−0.078** — the four-parameter problem is not degenerate, corroborating
the published −0.07 on the old model.

Calibration floors on Model C: n=5 → **12.30 %**, n=9 → **3.98 %**, n=17 → **2.97 %**.
The legacy figures (13.5 % / 4.9 %) were the old model's; magnitudes and the
"commission on at least two load events" conclusion both transfer.

**Recommended external wording:** *"On the IEC two-exponential structure, the
four-parameter classical estimator is unbiased to better than 0.12 % and sits on the
Cramér–Rao bound (0.97 / 1.01 / 0.95 / 0.97× the folded-Gaussian expectation, 400
seeds)."* Drop "0.99–1.02×" and "all four" — those were never measured together.

## Tolerance policy

Every regression comparison uses `tolerance = max(0.05 × |published|, 0.01)`.

The ±5 % is from the project brief. The 0.01 absolute floor covers the fact that
published values are quoted to 2 decimal places — a tolerance tighter than the
quoting precision of the reference would fail on the reference's own rounding. For a
published 2.59 the 5 % term (0.13) dominates and the floor never engages; it engages
only near zero, where 5 % of 0.02 K is 0.001 K.

Efficiency-ratio bands are derived from the statistic's sampling distribution
(1.0 ± 3·SE at the seed count used), not chosen to fit the answer.

**No tolerance in this suite was widened to make a failing test pass.** One assertion
was corrected during the build — an early draft of
`test_amplitude_parameters_are_cheap_rate_parameters_are_not` asserted τ_w's bound
exceeds Δθ_hr's by 10×; the measured ratio is 4.7×. The claim was wrong, not the
threshold, and the test now asserts what the data supports (τ_w is the hardest of the
four, by >4× over Δθ_hr and >30× over Δθ_or).
